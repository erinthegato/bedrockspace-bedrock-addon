# BedrockSpace — Advanced Rocketry style space addon for Bedrock

A Minecraft Bedrock addon (one behavior pack + one resource pack) that ports the
spirit of Advanced Rocketry into the End-based space dimension approach you asked
for: rockets, space stations, a space dimension, and a custom star/planet/sun
skybox that only appears in space.

## What it adds

- **Rocket** — craftable, fuelable block/item. When it enters the launching state
  and a player is nearby, the player is teleported into the space dimension at the
  default station orbit.
- **Astronaut suit** — chest armor item with a small defense bonus for space play.
- **Space station anchor** — block you can place in space; interacting with it docks
  the player to that station coordinate in the space dimension.
- **Space dimension** — `bedrockspace:space` uses a custom 6-face skybox texture
  (black background, stars, a planet and a sun) and near-black fog, so it reads as
  space. The skybox is only applied in the space dimension, not the overworld.
- **Fell out of space → overworld** — a script checks player position every time it
  changes while in the space dimension; if the player leaves the space build area
  (roughly -64..64 x/z, y 20..120) they are teleported back to
  `minecraft:overworld` at 0,100,0.
- **Custom skybox** — real PNG cubemap faces generated into the resource pack, plus
  rocket/station/astronaut textures and atlas registrations so the pack has visible
  art instead of placeholders.

## Bedrock limitations worth knowing

- Bedrock custom-skybox support is dimension-specific. The overworld sky is a
  cubemap at `textures/environment/overworld_cubemap/`, but that only affects the
  overworld. The practical way to have a space-only skybox is a custom dimension
  with its skybox declared in the dimension's `minecraft:environment.skybox`, which
  is what this addon does.
- Because Bedrock dimension behavior is mostly driven by `dimension.json` + the
  world template you start the world from, space is implemented as a dimension you
  enter via the rocket teleport, not as a fully automatic solar-system dimension
  (that full AR experience is beyond what Bedrock behavior packs can express).
- The "fall out of space" respawn guard is a script-side bounds check teleport,
  since Bedrock behavior packs cannot make a dimension literally fall-off-the-world
  kill you by default.

## Pack layout

- `BedrockSpace/BedrockSpace/behavior_pack/` — manifest, blocks, items, recipes,
  item catalog, scripts, and the space dimension definition.
- `BedrockSpace/BedrockSpace/resource_pack/` — manifest, textures (skybox cubemap +
  block/item PNGs + atlas JSONs), models, and texts.

## Installing

1. In Minecraft Bedrock, enable **both** packs on the world:
   - `BedrockSpace Core (Behavior)`
   - `BedrockSpace Core (Resources)`
2. The space dimension is reached by launching a rocket while near it, or by
   teleporting yourself into `bedrockspace:space` with commands while testing.
3. Use `/space` in the space dimension to place a space station anchor at your
   feet.

## Building the art

The resource-pack PNGs and atlas JSONs are generated by
`BedrockSpace/tools/gen_bedrockspace.py`. If you need to rebuild the art, run that
script from the project root.

## Scope notes

This is a functional bedrock-style port of the parts you asked for, not a full
Advanced Rocketry clone. Things like procedural planets, orbit mechanics, multiblock
rockets, satellite builders, railguns, terraforming and research are out of scope
for this pass.
